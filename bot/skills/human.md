# Human summary and cover letter

Load this skill for every application pack, before `save_application`. The professional
summary and the cover letter both go through this pass. Facts stay inside the master CV.

## Summary

- Two sentences, exactly 350 characters, so the one-page Canva frame still fits.
- No "I". It is a CV summary, written the way a person describes work they actually did.
- Lead with the part of Samvel's experience that matters for this posting.
- Prefer one concrete detail from the CV over a general claim.

## Cover letter

- At most two paragraphs, separated by one blank line. One paragraph is fine. Three is not.
- Put "Samvel Demirtshyan" at the end of the second paragraph. Do not give the name its own paragraph.
- Use "I". Address the hiring team at that company in the first sentence.
- Paragraph one: why this role at this company, tied to something real in the posting.
- Paragraph two: one proof from the CV that matches what they asked for, then a plain close.
- No bullets, headers, or subject line.

## Voice

Write like a careful email, not a brochure. Mix a short sentence with a longer one. If a
sentence could belong to any applicant, rewrite it with a detail from this CV.

Do not use: delve, leverage, robust, seamless, cutting-edge, passionate, thrilled, excited to,
honed, spearheaded, landscape, tapestry, pivotal, foster, utilize, "not only ... but",
"I am writing to express", or a stack of em dashes.

After the draft, read it once and cut anything that sounds generated. Do not add employers,
dates, metrics, tools, or outcomes that are not in the master CV.
